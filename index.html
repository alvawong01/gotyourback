How it works

Our AI analyses key skeletal landmarks to detect asymmetry in your shoulders, hips, and spine alignment — common early indicators of scoliosis.

Physio Exercise

Six exercises recommended for scoliosis management. The webcam counts reps or tracks hold time and checks your form in real time.

Pelvic Tilts

⏱ Timed hold2 sets × 10

Lie on your back with knees bent and feet flat. Gently flatten your lower back against the floor by tightening your abdominals and tilting your pelvis upward. Hold for 3 full breaths, then release. Strengthens the core and reduces lumbar curve.

Side Planks

⏱ 20–30 s holdEach side

Lie on one side and prop yourself up on your forearm, feet stacked. Lift your hips off the floor to form a straight line from head to feet. Hold for 20–30 seconds. Builds the lateral core muscles that support the spine and counteract spinal curves.

Arm & Leg Raises

🔢 15 reps

Lie on your front, forehead to the ground. Extend arms straight overhead, palms down. Keeping both legs straight, simultaneously raise your arms and legs off the ground. Hold for one full breath, then lower. Strengthens the posterior chain and spinal extensors.

Cat-Cow

🔢 2 sets × 10

Begin on hands and knees with a level back. Inhale and draw abdominals up, arching your spine toward the ceiling (Cat). Exhale and release, letting your belly drop while lifting your head toward the ceiling (Cow). Move slowly and breathe through each transition.

Latissimus Dorsi Stretch

🔢 5–10 reps/side

Stand with feet shoulder-width apart, knees slightly bent. Reach both hands overhead and grip the right wrist with the left hand. Gently bend toward the right until a stretch is felt along the left side. Hold 1–2 breaths, then return upright. Repeat on the opposite side.

Bird-Dog

⏱ 5 s hold10–15 reps/side

Begin on hands and knees, hands under shoulders, knees under hips. Extend one arm forward and the opposite leg straight back simultaneously, keeping your back flat. Hold for 5 seconds, breathing normally. Return and repeat on the other side. Improves spinal stability and balance.

Abdominal Press

⏱ 3-breath hold2 sets × 10

Lie on your back, feet flat, knees bent, back in a neutral position. Raise both feet until thighs and shins form a 90° angle, knees above hips. Press your hands against your knees while simultaneously pulling your knees toward your hands using your abdominal muscles — neither should move. Hold for 3 full breaths, then relax.

    Who We Are

    We are a community-driven initiative dedicated to improving scoliosis awareness, early detection, and access to care.

    Our platform bridges the gap between patients, families, and healthcare providers — making scoliosis support accessible to everyone, everywhere.

    Our Mission

    To empower individuals and families affected by scoliosis with the tools, knowledge, and community support they need to live full, active lives.

    A future where scoliosis is detected early and supported holistically — through technology, education, and compassionate care.
